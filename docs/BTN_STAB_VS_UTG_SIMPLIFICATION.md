# Approved STU004 BTN stab simplification

## Scope

This is the approved manual teaching table for `STU004 / 02_BTN_AFTER_CHECK`:
UTG checks and BTN chooses CHECK or BET one half-pot. It was reviewed from the
tracked combo/action-EV export and is not generated by a repository script.

Classifier priority remains OESD, Gutshot, made hand,
`2 overcards + BDFD`, then Air. The production generator, canonical workbook,
trainer and active STU005 solve were not changed.

## Flop classes

The three exhaustive classes contain all 286 study boards:

- `A-high`: 66 boards;
- `K/Q-high`: 100 boards;
- `J-high and lower`: 120 boards.

Relative to the former table only Two pair+ on K/Q-high and Gutshot on A-high
change from B/X to pure BET. Pure-betting K/Q-high Two pair+ removes the
dominant value-tail error. Pure-betting A-high Gutshot corrects the category
underbet and is especially supported by BDFD holdings.

Accepted reach-weighted row deviations are Two pair+ +14.4 pp, Overpair
-18.0 pp, OESD +10.6 pp and `2 overcards + BDFD` -10.7 pp. Overpair and
two-overcard BDFD remain B/X because pure BET would overshoot them more.

GPT-6 Astra independently supported the candidate. Local reproduction split
Two pair+ into two pair, set and completed straight, and split A-high Gutshot
by made-hand base and BDFD. The worst A-high Gutshot tail rises locally, but
the complete candidate halves the former branch maximum and improves every
reported aggregate audit measure.
