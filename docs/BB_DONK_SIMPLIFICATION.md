# Approved STU002 BB donk simplification

## Scope

This is the approved manual teaching table for `STU002 / 01_BB_FIRST`:
BB acts first and chooses CHECK or DONK BET one half-pot. It was reviewed from
the tracked combo/action-EV export and is not generated by a repository script.

The rule is limited to the 286 tracked unpaired study boards. Classifier
priority remains OESD, Gutshot, made hand, `2 overcards + BDFD`, then Air.
The production generator, canonical workbook, trainer and active STU005 solve
were not changed.

## Flop classes

- `[9-8]xx`: 36 boards with top rank 9 or 8;
- `[7-4]xx`: 20 boards with top rank 7 through 4;
- T-high and higher: always CHECK.

On 9/8-high, solver/candidate reach DONK is 14.52/15.00%. On 7-high and
lower it is 42.46/37.65%. Remaining row deviations are approximately
-8.1 pp for Two pair+ and -8.0 pp for `2 overcards + BDFD`.

A tested one-column `9-high and lower` version was rejected because its good
overall frequency hid opposing errors: approximately +10 pp on 9/8-high and
-11 pp on 7-high and lower. The approved second column removes that
cancellation at a small complexity cost.

GPT-6 Astra initially preferred one column, then revised its recommendation
after the local height and connectedness audit reproduced the opposing errors.
No separate connected/disconnected column is supported: both 9/8 subgroups
showed the same over-donk direction under the rejected merge.
